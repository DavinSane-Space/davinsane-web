CSF Compraventa, motorcycle catalog platform

01 Overview

CSF Compraventa is the group's motorcycle buying and selling unit. I designed the catalog platform and the admin panel for their inventory.

02 The Problem

They had no digital catalog of motorcycles available for sale. Every potential customer inquiry depended on manual communication.

03 Process

I reused the architecture already built for CSF Investments, adapting it to a motorcycle catalog instead of apartments. This allowed me to move much faster than starting from scratch.

Reused architecture, adapted from CSF Investments

04 Solution

I designed a public catalog site with a spec sheet per motorcycle (brand, model, year, engine size, mileage, condition, price) and photo gallery, along with a private admin panel so the team can upload and manage inventory directly.

Motorcycle spec sheet and photo gallery

05 Results

Proposal and architecture completed, ready for development, largely reusing the system already proven with CSF Investments.
