CSF Investments, real estate platform and admin panel

01 Overview

CSF Investments is the group's real estate unit: they buy off-plan apartments, complete them, and rent them out through an external property manager. I designed and built their complete digital platform.

02 The Problem

There was no site or system to showcase real estate projects or manage apartment inventory. Everything was handled manually.

03 Process

I reused the brand system already defined for Grupo CSF to speed up development. I designed a hierarchical structure where each project (building or condominium) can contain multiple individual apartment units, each with its own status (available, reserved, sold).

Project → apartment unit hierarchy and status model

04 Solution

I built a public site with an interactive project map, city-filterable cards, and a detailed view for each apartment with a photo gallery. I also built a full private admin panel where the client's team can create, edit, and manage projects and apartments without depending on me, including image uploads and secure authentication.

Admin panel, project and apartment management

05 Results

The site is live in production with a custom domain and real data. The CSF Investments team, including Juan Esteban with his own admin access, actively uses the panel to manage their day-to-day inventory.
