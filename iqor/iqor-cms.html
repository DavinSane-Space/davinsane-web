01 Context and problem

iQor is a global company with a lot of people who need to hear the same thing at the same time: announcements, articles, surveys, forms and courses. All of that lives in the HUB, the app employees use. But someone has to create it, review it and publish it, and that part didn't have a place of its own.

In practice, content was scattered across loose documents and several separate apps that weren't built for this. To see each piece, people had to open a different app and go through many steps.

The problem was building that place: a CMS where the people who produce content can write, approve and publish without friction, with consistent templates and visual themes, and where content reaches the HUB with the right structure. It had to be clear for people using it every day and flexible enough for so many different content types.

02 My role and the team

I was the Senior UX/UI Designer on the project. I designed the interface, the components and the handoff specs, and I used Replit AI and Claude AI to turn those designs into a working product. It took about eight weeks.

I worked with the product director, the Lead Product Owner, the DevOps director and the people who use the app, iQor's call center agents, who helped me understand what they needed day to day.
