About Me

I work at the point where design, code, and AI stop being separate disciplines. I've spent six years designing products people actually use. I plan every design thinking about how a product scales over time, how a user interacts with it intuitively, and I hand it off clearly and technically so the development team has no ambiguity. I also use an AI-driven workflow that speeds up the whole process, from idea to production. I don't just imagine what a product could be. I ship it.

I don't stop at Figma. I also work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Tailwind, and Supabase. That means I know what's technically feasible from day one, and I hand off designs the dev team can build without having to guess anything.

I use AI tools throughout my whole process, from early research to final implementation. It's not an extra step, it's just part of how I design today. It helps me move faster without cutting corners on quality.

Before I design a single screen, I make sure I understand what the person using the product actually needs. Every design decision I make considers the real impact it has, both for the user and for the business.

Besides designing, I spend time developing other designers. I've spent 3 years mentoring interns and junior designers, helping them build better judgment and grow in their process, not just get the day's task done.
